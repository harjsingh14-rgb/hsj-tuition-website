The ChemMastery Framework

Five pillars. One proven system.

Every student follows the same structured framework. Each pillar closes a specific gap between where you are now and where you need to be on exam day.

1

Initial Diagnostic

We start with a full diagnostic assessment covering content knowledge, exam technique and study habits. No assumptions and no guesswork. We identify exactly what is holding you back from Day 1.

2

Personalised Roadmap

Based on your diagnostic, you receive a weekly plan tailored to your exam board, target grade and timeline. You will always know exactly what to work on next and why it matters.

3

Topic Mastery

Clear, precise explanations using models, mechanisms and worked examples, taught the way examiners expect you to think. We bridge the gap between understanding a topic and being able to use it under pressure.

4

Weekly Past-Paper Training

Every session includes exam questions, mark scheme application and targeted feedback. You learn to express your knowledge precisely how examiners award marks because understanding alone does not guarantee grades.

5

Problem-Solving and High-Mark Strategies

Focused training on the question types most students avoid: multi-step calculations, complex mechanisms and extended responses. We work on your weak spots every week until they become strengths.
